## Secrets handling

We spent most of the hour on secrets. The question was whether credentials
should ever appear in documents we index, and the answer was no — but the
answer everyone actually wanted was what happens when they do anyway.

Today nothing happens. A document with an API key in it gets embedded like any
other, and the key is recoverable from the embedding afterwards. Scanning the
store later does not undo that.

Action: evaluate a scanner that runs before the embedding call rather than
after storage. Notes in the runbook once we have picked one.
